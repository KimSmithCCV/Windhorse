# Windhorse Vineyard — Brand Guide Reference

Source: `CCV_WV_BRAND_GUIDE-07.2026.pdf` (Capital Call Vintners, July 2026 Visual Identity Guidelines). Saved here as a text reference for building/updating the site — the original PDF should be added to this folder if a permanent copy is needed (only pages 1–6 were shared in chat; there may be additional pages covering full color palette and typography specs that aren't captured here).

## Brand personality

Windhorse Vineyard is contemporary, understated, and quietly luxurious — "complex but not complicated." The visual language favors simplicity, craftsmanship, and authenticity over ornamentation.

- Refined confidence
- Modern craftsmanship
- Vineyard authenticity
- Quiet luxury
- Precision and restraint

Positioning: a modern fine-wine brand rooted in authenticity, not embellishment — restrained typography, minimal graphic language, metallic foil execution.

## Logo elements

- **CCV Roundel** — the Capital Call Vintners circular seal mark. Acts as the brand's seal of quality/craftsmanship, representing shared stewardship of the vineyard.
- **Windhorse Vineyard logotype** — "WINDHORSE" set in a refined, high-contrast serif with classical proportions and generous letter spacing (sophistication, permanence, calm confidence). "VINEYARD" set below in a minimalist sans-serif with expanded tracking, as a contemporary counterbalance. The serif/sans-serif pairing is intentional: heritage vs. modernity.

## Logo lockups

- Vertical lockup: CCV roundel centered above the WINDHORSE / VINEYARD wordmark.
- Horizontal lockup: CCV roundel to the left of the wordmark.
- Clear space: measured in **X = cap height of the logotype**. Keep at least 1X clear space around the lockup on all sides; roundel diameter ≈ 3X; horizontal gap between roundel and wordmark ≈ 1-1/4X.
- Color variations: **Positive** (dark logotype/roundel on white) and **Reversed** (gold logotype/roundel on black), in both vertical and horizontal arrangements.
- On photography: a **simplified** version (wordmark + "COLUMBIA GORGE · OREGON" locality line only, in a thin-bordered box) or a **complex** version (adds the CCV roundel above the wordmark) — both centered over darker areas of the image for contrast, gold on a semi-dark overlay box.

## Logo usage rules (do not)

- Do not alter, redraw, or modify the logo in any way.
- Do not reposition, respace, or recreate the typographic elements.
- Do not place the identity on a background/texture without sufficient contrast for legibility.
- Do not violate clear-space requirements with headlines, text, or graphics.
- Use only authorized logo assets — check with Capital Call Vintners for approved files.

## Color notes (from available pages)

- Primary palette: black (label/background) with satin/metallic **gold** typography and linework.
- CCV roundel appears in gold (reversed/positive lockups) or a deep purple/plum tone (as seen on the Nero d'Avola label and portfolio bottle shots) — varietal color appears to shift by wine (e.g., purple, gold/mustard, red-orange rings seen across the bottle lineup), while the primary brand gold/black remains constant for the wordmark and general identity.
- Full color specs (hex/CMYK values) were not included in the pages shared — request the complete PDF or a later page range if exact color codes are needed for CSS.

## Relevance to the website build

- Current site (`index.html` / `styles.css`) uses a Playfair Display (serif) + Manrope (sans) + DM Mono pairing — Playfair Display is a reasonable stand-in for the brand's classical serif wordmark treatment, and Manrope covers the expanded-tracking sans-serif feel for "VINEYARD"-style subheads.
- If exact brand fonts/colors are proprietary (custom-drawn logotype, specific gold hex), the actual logo should be used as an image asset rather than recreated in CSS text, per the "do not redraw" rule above.
